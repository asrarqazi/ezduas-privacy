EZ Duas Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

EZ Duas is designed to work privately and offline.

Data Collection

EZ Duas does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits.

Data Stored on Your Device

EZ Duas stores favorites, personal collection names and membership, appearance settings, reading preferences, and category-menu preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's features and is not transmitted to ISSA Codeworks.

You can remove this locally stored information by deleting the app from your device.

Sharing

If you choose to share a dua image, the app creates the image on your device and presents the system share sheet. The service or person you select may process the shared image according to their own privacy practices. EZ Duas does not receive information about your sharing activity.

External Links

EZ Duas includes links to source material and the ISSA Codeworks website. Opening an external link leaves the app, and the destination website's privacy practices apply.

Children

EZ Duas does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@issacodeworks.com.
